# 🎧 MusicProWorkshop

**A community where musicians help each other get better.**

[MusicProWorkshop.com](https://musicproworkshop.com) is a place to share your tracks, get honest and useful feedback from other musicians, and pay it forward by reviewing theirs. Whether you're polishing a mix, testing a new song idea, or just want a fresh set of ears, you'll find people who care about the craft as much as you do.

## 🎶 What you can do

- **Share your music**: upload a track and ask for the kind of feedback you need
- **Get real feedback**: thoughtful input from fellow musicians, not just likes
- **Give back**: review other artists' work and sharpen your own ear along the way
- **Grow together**: connect with musicians who are working toward the same goals

## 🛠️ About this organization

This is where the code behind MusicProWorkshop.com lives. Have an idea, found a bug, or want to contribute? Open an issue or start a discussion in the relevant repo.
